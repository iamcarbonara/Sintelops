Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

How do you differ from traditional management consulting?

We specialize in operational execution. While traditional consulting produces strategic plans and theoretical models, our mandate acts on reality: corporate governance. We eliminate bottlenecks and align departments (sales, operations, accounting) so that leadership decisions are executed without friction, delays, or information loss.

Why do you focus on companies with over 50 employees?

It's generally past the threshold of 50 employees and €50M in revenue that organic complexity explodes. At that scale, managerial expertise and the entrepreneur's personal touch are no longer enough to keep communication coordinated, and severe "disconnects" emerge between what's decided and what's actually executed across operational units. That's exactly where we intervene.

Do you build or implement ERP/management software?

We don't operate as pure IT system integrators nor develop custom scripts, but we strongly focus on decisional technology infrastructure. We help streamline chaotic tools and support you in adopting a sensible software ecosystem where information flows automatically from Department A to Department B, ensuring lean reporting and perfect data control.

How long does the initial Operational Diagnosis take?

The first phase, called "Diagnosis," typically takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on the complexity of the status quo. Our goal isn't to produce lengthy unreachable analyses but to extract a Prioritized Action Plan quickly to promptly resolve growth bottlenecks and generate a drastic impact on daily operations.

Can we end the engagement after the Diagnostic Phase with no commitments?

Yes, the Operational Diagnosis delivers immediate, tangible output (the Action Plan). You can absolutely choose to conclude the relationship after the Diagnostic Report and handle the redesign internally, confident that no budget was wasted without long-term commitments. This is a choice protected by our operating protocols.
