Before redesigning anything, we understand how your company actually works. We map the real frictions — not theoretical ones — between leadership, departments, and operations.

What it is

An infrastructure x-ray in 2-4 weeks

The Operational Diagnosis is a structured, non-invasive analysis that maps how your company actually functions compared to how it should. We identify bottlenecks, decision disconnects, and coordination gaps that slow down execution — and we deliver a concrete, prioritized Action Plan.

What we analyze

The four diagnostic dimensions

01

Decision flows

How decisions actually move through the organization — who decides what, how fast, and with what information.

02

Inter-departmental coordination

How functions talk to each other — handoffs, shared processes, and where information gets lost.

03

Technology infrastructure

How digital tools are used — whether they support or hinder actual operational flows.

04

Leadership overload

How much the CEO/Founder is operationally trapped — and what can be structurally delegated.

Output

What you receive at the end

I

Diagnostic Report

A clear document that maps all identified frictions with severity level and business impact.

II

Prioritized Action Plan

A practical roadmap with interventions ranked by impact and implementation urgency.

III

Full autonomy

You can proceed independently or continue with us. No lock-in, no hidden clauses.
